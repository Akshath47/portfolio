# Knowledge Base Template Guide

This document provides templates for creating knowledge base files for the AI chatbot.

---

## File Naming Convention

Use kebab-case (lowercase with hyphens):
- ✅ `valuelabs-ai-internship.md`
- ✅ `programming-languages.md`
- ❌ `ValueLabs AI Internship.md`
- ❌ `programming_languages.md`

---

## Template 1: Experience/Work Entry

```markdown
---
title: "Position at Company Name"
category: "experience"
tags: ["work", "internship", "company-name", "technology-1", "technology-2"]
priority: 8
last_updated: "2025-01-01"
---

# [Position Title] at [Company Name]

**Duration:** [Start Month Year] – [End Month Year]
**Location:** [City, Country]
**Type:** [Full-time/Internship/Part-time/Volunteer]

## Overview
Brief summary of the role and main focus areas (2-3 sentences).

## Key Responsibilities
- Responsibility 1: Detailed description with technologies/tools used
- Responsibility 2: Include metrics, outcomes, or impact where possible
- Responsibility 3: Specific projects or initiatives led

## Technical Stack
- **Languages:** Python, JavaScript, etc.
- **Frameworks:** React, Next.js, etc.
- **Tools:** Qdrant, Docker, Git, etc.
- **Methodologies:** Agile, CI/CD, etc.

## Key Achievements
- Achievement 1 with measurable impact (e.g., "Reduced response time from 90s to 6s")
- Achievement 2 with specific outcomes
- Achievement 3 highlighting innovation or problem-solving

## Projects & Initiatives
### Project 1 Name
Brief description of the project, your role, and the outcome.

### Project 2 Name
Brief description of the project, your role, and the outcome.

## Skills Developed
- Technical skill 1
- Technical skill 2
- Soft skill 1
- Soft skill 2

## Challenges & Solutions
**Challenge:** Description of a significant challenge faced
**Solution:** How you approached and solved it
**Outcome:** Results and learnings

## Learnings & Takeaways
Key insights and experiences gained from this role.
```

## Writing Guidelines

### DO's
✅ Write in first person for personal content
✅ Be specific with numbers, metrics, and outcomes
✅ Use clear, simple language
✅ Include context for technical terms
✅ Add examples and use cases
✅ Keep content focused on one topic per file
✅ Update regularly with new information

### DON'Ts
❌ Use vague or generic descriptions
❌ Include sensitive personal information
❌ Write extremely long paragraphs
❌ Use jargon without explanation
❌ Duplicate information across files
❌ Include unverified or exaggerated claims

---

## Content Organization Tips

1. **One Topic Per File:** Each file should cover one specific aspect
2. **Cross-Reference:** Mention related files/topics for better context
3. **Keep Updated:** Review and update files quarterly
4. **Be Authentic:** Write in your natural voice
5. **Include Examples:** Real examples make content more valuable
6. **Measure Impact:** Include quantifiable achievements where possible

---

## Sample Questions Your Chatbot Should Answer

After creating your knowledge base, test with these questions:

**About You:**
- "Who are you?"
- "Tell me about your background"
- "What are you passionate about?"

**Experience:**
- "What experience do you have with AI/ML?"
- "Tell me about your internship at X company"
- "What challenges have you solved?"

**Projects:**
- "What projects have you built?"
- "Tell me about [specific project name]"
- "What's your most impressive project?"

**Skills:**
- "What programming languages do you know?"
- "How experienced are you with [technology]?"
- "What tools and frameworks do you use?"

**Education:**
- "Where do you study?"
- "What courses have you taken?"
- "Tell me about your academic projects"

**Availability:**
- "How can I contact you?"
- "Are you looking for opportunities?"
- "What kind of work are you interested in?"
